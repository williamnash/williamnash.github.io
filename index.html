William Nash

Head of Engineering at Chloris Geospatial

I build software for measuring the physical world. Today that means petabyte-scale satellite data and machine learning that map forest carbon across the planet. Before that, I spent six years in particle physics at UCLA and CERN, and two writing beam-control software for proton cancer therapy.
